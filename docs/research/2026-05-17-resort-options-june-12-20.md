# Resort Research — June 12-20, 2026

**Researched:** 2026-05-17
**Method:** 4 parallel research agents (Caribbean/Mexico, SE Asia, Europe/Med, USA/Americas) using WebSearch + WebFetch

## Requirements
- Dates: June 12-20, 2026 (8 nights window; 5-night minimum acceptable)
- Budget: **Under $2,000 USD TOTAL** for 2 adults (lodging + included food/drinks)
- Coverage: All-inclusive OR everything-included
- Beds: **Two separate beds** (twin/twin or double/double, NOT one king)
- Flights NOT included in budget
- Anywhere in the world

## TOP PICKS — Final Ranked Shortlist

Ranked by value × quality × confidence that bed/AI/dates check out.

### 1. Trakia Plaza Hotel — Sunny Beach, Bulgaria (4-star)
- **Total: ~$560-720 for 8 nights** (full AI, 2 adults)
- Cheapest true all-inclusive in the comparison. Buffet meals, local alcohol + non-alcohol drinks, snacks, pool, entertainment. Twin or double bed config available.
- **Best for:** Lowest total cost, leaves ~$1,300 for excursions/spending
- Source: [Trakia Plaza official](https://trakiaplaza.eu/all-inclusive/) · [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Sunny-Beach-Hotels-Trakia-Plaza-Hotel.h19098057.Hotel-Information)

### 2. Hoi An, Vietnam — Vignette Moire / Ahoy Hoi An (4-star)
- **Total: ~$640-960 for 8 nights** (room + breakfast; meals out are $10-15/day)
- June is Hoi An's driest, sunniest month — best weather of any option on this list. Both properties explicitly offer twin-bed rooms.
- **Best for:** Best weather + culture + price combo. Not a "resort" in the all-inclusive sense — more boutique resort + cheap food scene.
- Source: [IHG Moire Hoi An](https://www.ihg.com/vignettecollection/hotels/us/en/hoi-an/dadho/hoteldetail) · [Tripadvisor Ahoy](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g298082-d25226476-Reviews-Ahoy_Hoi_An_Boutique_Resort_Spa-Hoi_An_Quang_Nam_Province.html)

### 3. Iberostar Waves Averroes — Yasmine Hammamet, Tunisia (4-star)
- **Total: ~$840-1,000 for 7 nights** (full AI, 2 adults)
- Real branded all-inclusive: 3 meals, local drinks, snacks, beach, watersports, entertainment. Twin beds on request.
- **Best for:** True branded AI experience at low cost on a Mediterranean beach.
- Source: [Iberostar Averroes](https://www.iberostar.com/en/hotels/hammamet/iberostar-waves-averroes/) · [On The Beach](https://www.onthebeach.co.uk/hotels/tunisia/hammamet/iberostar-waves-averroes-hotel)

### 4. New World Phu Quoc Resort — Phu Quoc Island, Vietnam (5-star)
- **Total: ~$900-1,400 for 8 nights** (breakfast included; rates ~$110-170/night in June low season)
- 5-star beachfront, free water park, twin rooms on request. June is wet season — afternoon showers but mornings typically clear.
- Source: [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Phu-Quoc-Hotels-New-World-Phu-Quoc-Resort.h66527269.Hotel-Information) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/resorts/city/vn/phu-quoc.html)

### 5. Club Hotel Turan Prince World — Side, Antalya, Turkey (5-star)
- **Total: ~$1,060+ for 7 nights** (Ultra AI, 2 adults — hotel-only portion)
- Ultra all-inclusive: multiple restaurants, all drinks incl. premium spirits, water park, private beach, entertainment. Twin standard in double rooms.
- **Best for:** 5-star ultra-AI experience well under budget.
- Source: [On The Beach Antalya](https://www.onthebeach.co.uk/holidays/all-inclusive-holidays/turkey/antalya) · [TUI Turkey](https://www.tui.co.uk/destinations/europe/turkey/turkey-antalya/holidays-turkey-antalya/all-inclusive-holidays)

### 6. JAZ Fanara Resort — Sharm El Sheikh, Egypt (4-star)
- **Total: ~$1,170 for 7 nights** (full AI; Sharm AI avg $167/night)
- Full AI on the Red Sea with house reef snorkeling included. Twin standard. June is shoulder pricing in Sharm.
- Source: [Thomas Cook Sharm AI](https://www.thomascook.com/holidays/egypt/red-sea/sharm-el-sheikh/all-inclusive) · [Orbitz Sharm AI](https://www.orbitz.com/Sharm-El-Sheikh-Hotels-All-Inclusive-Hotel.0-0-d3274-tAllinclusiveHotel.Travel-Guide-Filter-Hotels)

### 7. Whala!Bavaro — Bavaro, Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (3-star)
- **Total: ~$1,200-1,400 for 8 nights** (full AI; KAYAK from $84/night)
- Cheapest solid Caribbean AI for these dates. 3 meals, 3 bars, 3 pools, beach. No premium liquor. Double rooms with two beds standard.
- **Best for:** True Caribbean beach AI at the lowest price.
- Source: [KAYAK Whala!Bavaro](https://www.kayak.com/Punta-Cana-Hotels-whala-bavaro.622665.ksp)

### 8. Riu Naiboa — Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (4-star)
- **Total: ~$1,300-1,700 for 8 nights** (full Riu 24-hour AI)
- Riu official site **explicitly confirms** Double Standard rooms have two small double beds (120x200 cm) — strongest bed-config confidence on this list. 24hr AI with premium drinks; shares Riu Bambu's beach.
- Source: [Riu Naiboa official](https://www.riu.com/en/hotel/dominican-republic/punta-cana/hotel-riu-naiboa/index.jsp) · [BeachResort.net](https://riunaiboa.beachresort.net/)

### 9. JAZ Aquamarine Resort — Hurghada, Egypt (5-star)
- **Total: ~$1,200-1,520 for 8 nights** (full AI; Hurghada AI avg $191/night)

- Source: [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Hurghada-Hotels-Jaz-Aquamarine-Resort-All-Inclusive.h3032478.Hotel-Information) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/all-inclusive/city/eg/al-ghardaqah.html)

- Stronger beach than Punta Cana proper (Bayahibe). "Spacious Double" room offers two double beds. Meals, drinks (some premium), watersports, kids' club, shows.
- Source: [Wyndham Vivaresorts](https://vivaresortsbywyndham.com/) · [KAYAK Viva Dominicus](https://www.kayak.com/La-Romana-Hotels-Viva-Wyndham-Dominicus-Palace.158238.ksp)

### 12. Grand Sirenis Punta Cana Resort & Aquagames — Uvero Alto, DR (5-star value)
- **Total: ~$1,600-1,950 for 8 nights** (full AI; tight to budget)
- 7+ themed restaurants, water park, premium drinks, dive center. Two-double-bed rooms confirmed via official site.
- Source: [Sirenis Hotels](https://www.sirenishotels.com/en/punta-cana-hotels/grand-sirenis-punta-cana-resort/) · [CheapCaribbean](https://www.cheapcaribbean.com/resorts/grand-sirenis-punta-cana-all-inclusive/)

## USA / Reality Check

A true 8-night USA beach all-inclusive under $2k for 2 adults in June **essentially does not exist** — June is peak family season and most USA AIs run $300-500+/night. Realistic USA options:

- **MGM Luxor / Excalibur AI package (Vegas)** — ~$1,320-1,700 / 8 nights for 2 (~$165/night). 2 queen beds, 3 meals/day, drinks, show tickets, resort fees. [Source](https://www.visitlasvegas.com/experience/post/all-inclusive-las-vegas-packages/)
- **Westgate Smoky Mountain 2BR Villa (Gatlinburg, TN)** — ~$800-1,300 / 8 nights, full kitchen + included water park; not AI but $700 leftover easily covers groceries. [Source](https://www.kayak.com/Gatlinburg-Hotels-Westgate-Smoky-Mountain-Resort-Spa.155878.ksp)
- **Bolongo Bay (St. Thomas, USVI)** — only true USVI AI; **requires cutting to 5 nights** (~$1,750-1,825) to fit budget. [Source](https://bolongobay.com/st-thomas-all-inclusive-beachfront-resort-vacations-packages/)
- **Club Med Sandpiper Bay (FL)** — would be the closest USA equivalent but $2,700-3,840 / 8 nights — **over budget**.

## Eliminated / Closed
- **Bahia Principe Grand Punta Cana** — full renovation Apr 10 - Nov 30, 2026 (closed for these dates).
- **Majestic Colonial Punta Cana** — closed Apr 6 - Nov 11, 2026.
- **Tyler Place (VT), Woodloch Pines (PA), Club Med Sandpiper Bay** — all over budget in June.
- **Greece / Spain / Croatia / Cyprus AI** — June is high season, typically exceeds $2k hotel-only for 7+ nights.
- **Cuba (Varadero)** — fits budget on price (~$80-120/night AI) but US-travel legal/payment complications.

## Bed-Config Caveat

All pricing assumes "Standard Double" / "Twin Room" categories. In DR/Mexico, junior suites usually default to a king — **specifically request "two double beds" / "habitación doble" / "twin beds" at booking** and get it in writing. Riu Naiboa is the only property on this list with the official site explicitly confirming the two-bed config.

## Pricing Confidence

All totals are aggregator "from" rates and brand averages, NOT live booked quotes for the exact June 12-20, 2026 window. Expect:
- ±15-25% variance vs. actual quote
- Additional $50-100 in resort fees/taxes on some bookings
- Lower prices via package operators (TUI, On The Beach, CheapCaribbean) than direct booking, especially for Turkey/Egypt/Tunisia.
