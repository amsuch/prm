# Resort Research — June 12-20, 2026

**Researched:** 2026-05-17
**Method:** 4 parallel research agents (Caribbean/Mexico, SE Asia, Europe/Med, USA/Americas) using WebSearch + WebFetch

## Requirements
- Dates: June 12-20, 2026 (8 nights window; 5-night minimum acceptable)
- Budget: **Under $2,000 USD TOTAL** for 2 adults (lodging + included food/drinks)
- Coverage: All-inclusive OR everything-included
- Beds: **Two separate beds** (twin/twin or double/double, NOT one king)
- Flights NOT included in budget
- Anywhere in the world

## TOP PICKS — Final Ranked Shortlist

Ranked by value × quality × confidence that bed/AI/dates check out.

### 1. Trakia Plaza Hotel — Sunny Beach, Bulgaria (4-star)
- **Total: ~$560-720 for 8 nights** (full AI, 2 adults)
- Cheapest true all-inclusive in the comparison. Buffet meals, local alcohol + non-alcohol drinks, snacks, pool, entertainment. Twin or double bed config available.
- **Best for:** Lowest total cost, leaves ~$1,300 for excursions/spending
- Source: [Trakia Plaza official](https://trakiaplaza.eu/all-inclusive/) · [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Sunny-Beach-Hotels-Trakia-Plaza-Hotel.h19098057.Hotel-Information)

### 2. Hoi An, Vietnam — Vignette Moire / Ahoy Hoi An (4-star)
- **Total: ~$640-960 for 8 nights** (room + breakfast; meals out are $10-15/day)
- June is Hoi An's driest, sunniest month — best weather of any option on this list. Both properties explicitly offer twin-bed rooms.
- **Best for:** Best weather + culture + price combo. Not a "resort" in the all-inclusive sense — more boutique resort + cheap food scene.
- Source: [IHG Moire Hoi An](https://www.ihg.com/vignettecollection/hotels/us/en/hoi-an/dadho/hoteldetail) · [Tripadvisor Ahoy](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g298082-d25226476-Reviews-Ahoy_Hoi_An_Boutique_Resort_Spa-Hoi_An_Quang_Nam_Province.html)

### 3. Iberostar Waves Averroes — Yasmine Hammamet, Tunisia (4-star)
- **Total: ~$840-1,000 for 7 nights** (full AI, 2 adults)
- Real branded all-inclusive: 3 meals, local drinks, snacks, beach, watersports, entertainment. Twin beds on request.
- **Best for:** True branded AI experience at low cost on a Mediterranean beach.
- Source: [Iberostar Averroes](https://www.iberostar.com/en/hotels/hammamet/iberostar-waves-averroes/) · [On The Beach](https://www.onthebeach.co.uk/hotels/tunisia/hammamet/iberostar-waves-averroes-hotel)

### 4. New World Phu Quoc Resort — Phu Quoc Island, Vietnam (5-star)

- Source: [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Phu-Quoc-Hotels-New-World-Phu-Quoc-Resort.h66527269.Hotel-Information) · [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/resorts/city/vn/phu-quoc.html)

## USA / Reality Check

A true 8-night USA beach all-inclusive under $2k for 2 adults in June **essentially does not exist** — June is peak family season and most USA AIs run $300-500+/night. Realistic USA options:

- **MGM Luxor / Excalibur AI package (Vegas)** — ~$1,320-1,700 / 8 nights for 2 (~$165/night). 2 queen beds, 3 meals/day, drinks, show tickets, resort fees. [Source](https://www.visitlasvegas.com/experience/post/all-inclusive-las-vegas-packages/)
- **Westgate Smoky Mountain 2BR Villa (Gatlinburg, TN)** — ~$800-1,300 / 8 nights, full kitchen + included water park; not AI but $700 leftover easily covers groceries. [Source](https://www.kayak.com/Gatlinburg-Hotels-Westgate-Smoky-Mountain-Resort-Spa.155878.ksp)
- **Bolongo Bay (St. Thomas, USVI)** — only true USVI AI; **requires cutting to 5 nights** (~$1,750-1,825) to fit budget. [Source](https://bolongobay.com/st-thomas-all-inclusive-beachfront-resort-vacations-packages/)
- **Club Med Sandpiper Bay (FL)** — would be the closest USA equivalent but $2,700-3,840 / 8 nights — **over budget**.

## Eliminated / Closed
- **Bahia Principe Grand Punta Cana** — full renovation Apr 10 - Nov 30, 2026 (closed for these dates).
- **Majestic Colonial Punta Cana** — closed Apr 6 - Nov 11, 2026.
- **Tyler Place (VT), Woodloch Pines (PA), Club Med Sandpiper Bay** — all over budget in June.

## Bed-Config Caveat

All pricing assumes "Standard Double" / "Twin Room" categories. In DR/Mexico, junior suites usually default to a king — **specifically request "two double beds" / "habitación doble" / "twin beds" at booking** and get it in writing. Riu Naiboa is the only property on this list with the official site explicitly confirming the two-bed config.

## Pricing Confidence

All totals are aggregator "from" rates and brand averages, NOT live booked quotes for the exact June 12-20, 2026 window. Expect:
- ±15-25% variance vs. actual quote
- Additional $50-100 in resort fees/taxes on some bookings
- Lower prices via package operators (TUI, On The Beach, CheapCaribbean) than direct booking, especially for Turkey/Egypt/Tunisia.

# Addendum — Obscure USA Locations (Maine, Oregon, Colorado, Utah, NM, ID, WA, NY)

Second-pass research focused on off-the-beaten-path domestic options. **Key insight:** Mid-June is genuine off-season at western ski-town condos AND pre-peak shoulder at Maine sporting camps / PNW lakes. The TRUE all-inclusive USA experience (Western dude ranches) does NOT fit budget — Vista Verde, C Lazy U, Lost Valley all start at $5k-$7k+ for 2 adults/week. The winning USA strategy is **off-season ski-town condo or cabin resort + groceries**.

## TOP USA OBSCURE PICKS — Ranked

### 1. Crested Butte, CO — 2BR Condo (most obscure + cheapest)
- **Total: ~$912-1,160 for 8 nights** ($114-145/night); ~$500 grocery budget → all-in **~$1,400-1,700**
- Wildflower capital of CO (late June peak), Victorian mining town, way less touristy than Aspen/Vail
- Full kitchen + Clark's Market in town. Many 2BR/2BA units with separate beds.
- Source: [KAYAK Crested Butte Rentals](https://www.kayak.com/Crested-Butte-Vacation-Rentals.11401.rental.ksp)

### 2. Steamboat Springs, CO — 2BR Condo
- **Total: ~$1,048-1,160 for 8 nights** ($131-145/night) + ~$500 groceries → **~$1,550-1,700 all-in**
- Strawberry Park Hot Springs, Yampa River tubing, Fish Creek Falls. June 6-13 is cheapest week of the year.
- 2BR condos with queen+queen or queen+2 twins widely available.
- Source: [KAYAK Steamboat Rentals](https://www.kayak.com/Steamboat-Springs-Vacation-Rentals.34792.rental.ksp)

### 3. Winter Park, CO — 2BR Condo
- **Total: ~$888-1,200 for 8 nights** ($111-150/night) + groceries
- Trestle Bike Park opens mid-June, Rocky Mountain NP 1hr away, Hot Sulphur Springs 30 min.
- Source: [Travelocity Winter Park Condos from $111](https://www.travelocity.com/Winter-Park-Condos.d10819-aaCondos.Travel-Guide-Accommodation)

### 4. Sipapu Resort, NM — Duplex Cabin (only true "resort" feel in budget)
- **Total: ~$960-1,440 for 8 nights** ($120-180/night, call to confirm)
- A genuine resort: on-site fly-fishing pond, river access, hiking, lift-served mountain biking. Near Taos. The most obscure pick on this list.
- Large Duplex sleeps 2-8 with multiple beds; Suite has king + twin bunks.
- Source: [Sipapu Lodging](https://www.sipapu.ski/lodging/)

### 5. Pagosa Springs, CO — 2BR Cabin (hot springs bonus)
- **Total: ~$800-1,320 for 8 nights** (target sub-$130/night)
- Walking distance to Pagosa Hot Springs / The Springs Resort, Wolf Creek wildflowers, San Juan NF.
- 2BR cabins with queens/twins. Full kitchen.
- Source: [Travelocity Pagosa Cabins](https://www.travelocity.com/Pagosa-Springs-Cabins.d9397-aaCabins.Travel-Guide-Accommodation) · [Fireside Cabins](https://firesidecabins.com/cabins.cfm)

### 6. Sol Duc Hot Springs Resort, WA — Olympic NP
- **Total: ~$1,570 + tax (~$1,700) for 8 nights** in standard cabin
- **Hot springs pool access INCLUDED in nightly rate** — closest thing to "all-inclusive feel" on this list.
- 2 queen beds confirmed in standard cabin. Kitchenette cabins available (+$30) — recommend for budget.
- Old-growth rainforest, mineral springs, NP access.
- Source: [Sol Duc Rates](https://www.nationalparkreservations.com/lodge/olympic-sol-duc-hot-springs-resort/rooms/)

### 7. Lake Quinault Lodge, WA — Olympic Peninsula
- **Total: ~$1,400-1,600 for 8 nights** (lakeside rooms $160-200/night, 25% advance discount)
- **"Two Double Beds" room type confirmed** in main lodge.
- 1926 historic Roosevelt-era lodge, indoor pool, sauna, kayaks/canoes on lake.
- Weather flag: rainforest = expect rain even in June.
- Source: [Lake Quinault Specials](https://www.olympicnationalparks.com/special-offers/specials-packages/lake-quinault-lodge/)

### 8. Hill's Resort, Priest Lake, ID
- **Total: ~$2,080 for 8 nights** OR **~$1,560 for 6 nights** (June 12-20 may fall in off-season tier through June 26)
- Family-run since 1946. Sandy lake beach, kayaks/SUP, restaurant, hiking. Truly rarely on tourist radar.
- 2-bedroom forest cabins and lakefront condos confirmed.
- Source: [Hill's Resort Rates](https://www.hillsresort.com/lodging/rates-and-policies/) — **call (208) 443-2551 to confirm tier**

### 9. Castine Cottages, Maine — Penobscot Bay
- **Total: ~$1,744 for 8 nights** (weekly rate at ~$200/night)
- Six secluded housekeeping cottages, 800 ft off-road, private beachfront, fully equipped kitchens (including lobster pot).
- 2 separate bedrooms confirmed — **bed type (twin vs double) requires email/phone confirmation**.
- Walkable historic Castine village. Quintessential Maine coast without Bar Harbor crowds.
- Source: [castinecottages.com/rates](https://www.castinecottages.com/rates.html)

### 10. Cabins at Mazama Village, Crater Lake NP, Oregon
- **Total: ~$1,600 + tax (~$1,720) for 8 nights** ($200/night) — or 5 nights for $1,000
- **2 queen beds confirmed**, private bath. Inside a national park.
- No kitchen in cabin; small store + restaurant on-site. Bring/drive for groceries.
- Weather flag: cool/possibly snowy at 6,000 ft in mid-June.
- Source: [Explore Crater Lake — Mazama Cabins](https://explorecraterlake.com/stay-at-crater-lake/the-cabins-at-mazama-village/) — books out a year ahead

### 11. White Pine Camp, Paul Smiths, NY — Adirondacks
- **Total: ~$1,120-1,600 for 8 nights** (Osgood cabin $140-200/night, spring tier through 6/26/26)
- Historic Adirondack Great Camp; the actual 1926 Coolidge Summer White House cabin.
- Tennis house, Japanese tea house, bowling alley, canoes on Osgood Pond. Kitchenettes (self-cater).
- Source: [whitepinecamp.com/rates](https://www.whitepinecamp.com/whitepinerates.html)

### 12. Sunriver Resort area, OR — 2BR Condo
- **Total: ~$1,500 for 8 nights** if you stack the "Stay 4 Pay 2" promo (valid through June 12 — shift dates to June 8-15 to maximize)
- Full kitchen, SHARC water park access, 28 mi paved bike trails, Deschutes River.
- Source: [Mountain Resort Properties Specials](https://www.mtresort.com/specials)

## Eliminated — Over Budget

- **ALL true Western dude ranches** (Vista Verde, C Lazy U, Lost Valley, Bar Lazy J, Sundance Trail, Cherokee Park, Drowsy Water) — $5k-$7k+ per couple per week even on early-season discount. The full-AI Western experience is out of reach.
- **Whitefish MT, Park City UT, Sundance Resort UT, Jackson WY, Bishop's Lodge NM** — $300-500+/night.
- **Triple Creek Ranch MT, Brush Creek Ranch WY** — multi-thousand per night AI.
- **Shore Lodge McCall, Sun Mountain Lodge WA** — $260-270+/night.

## USA Bottom Line

**Best obscure USA value:** Crested Butte or Steamboat 2BR condo (~$1,500 all-in for 8 nights including groceries). Both have separate bedrooms, off-season pricing, and serious natural beauty in June. **Best "resort feel":** Sol Duc Hot Springs (~$1,700, hot springs included) or Sipapu NM. **Best Maine coast pick:** Castine Cottages (~$1,744).
